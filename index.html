Reference Dosing Ranges & Clinical Scenarios

Evidence-based pharmacopeia guidelines and scenario-specific hemodynamic targets.

Pharmacological Reference for:
Noradrenaline (Norepinephrine)
Standard Unit:
mcg/kg/min
Standard Dosing Range
0.01 - 3.0 mcg/kg/min

Initial start rate: 0.05 - 0.1 mcg/kg/min. Titrate every 2-5 minutes to maintain target MAP > 65 mmHg.

Scenario Variation
Septic Shock vs Routine Hypovolemic Shock

Routine Hypovolemic / Vasodilatory Shock: 0.02 - 0.5 mcg/kg/min after adequate intravascular volume loading.
Septic Shock (Surviving Sepsis): Target MAP ≥ 65 mmHg. Escalation to 0.25 - 1.0+ mcg/kg/min. Add Vasopressin 0.03 U/min if refractory.

Safety & Precautions
  • Central venous catheter preferred; peripheral extravasation causes ischemic necrosis.
  • Monitor invasive arterial blood pressure line and cardiac rhythm continuously.
