Clinical Dosage Guidance & Special Protocols

Pharmacopeia Reference
Standard Dose Range
0.01 - 3.0 mcg/kg/min

Titrate every 2-5 minutes to maintain target MAP > 65 mmHg.

Special Conditions (Septic Shock)
0.25 - 1.0+ mcg/kg/min

Surviving Sepsis Campaign first choice vasopressor. Add Vasopressin 0.03 U/min if Noradrenaline requirement exceeds 0.25 mcg/kg/min.

Anaesthetic & Safety Warnings
  • Central line strongly preferred; risk of severe extravasation necrosis.
  • Potent peripheral alpha-1 vasoconstriction with mild beta-1 inotropic effect.
